# Source material

Original scans and photos for future transcription live here and are not part of the Astro public directory.

- `nlp/` — handwritten NLP material
- `generative-models/` — handwritten generative-models material

The [Generative Models transcription index](generative-models/README.md) maps the 44-page seminar scan to topics and tracks completed notes, technical corrections and remaining work.

Workflow: scan → transcribe → reproduce informative sketches → review → add an MDX note under `src/content/notes/`.

Keep diagrams next to the explanation they support. Redraw source sketches with labeled arrows, readable axes and captions; identify illustrative additions in the note's source section. Use vector diagrams and mathematically generated curves so they remain sharp and adapt to the article width.
