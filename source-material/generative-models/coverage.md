# Generative Models PDF coverage audit

Reviewed on 2026-10-02 against the archived, unmodified [44-page seminar scan](gen-models-seminars.pdf). Page numbers below are 1-based PDF pages. The audit compares the source with the sixteen English articles and their figure components, rather than inferring coverage from the syllabus labels.

## Findings

The VAE derivation was already present in [Variational Autoencoders](../../src/content/notes/generative-models/vae.mdx): evidence integral, insertion of the approximate posterior, expectation, Jensen inequality, reconstruction-minus-prior-KL objective and posterior-gap identity. The overview fix uses explicit article and section references.

The audit restored smaller source details:

- Gaussian inverse standardization; O/X entropy interpretation; the 0.99-biased reality versus fair-model example and both directed cross-entropies in nats.
- The density-MSE scale remark in its distribution-comparison context, and the sparse-AE architecture distinction and presenter caution.

- Page 25's Fenchel supporting-line reminder, with the conjugate-versus-slope shorthand corrected.
- Page 26's random-variable support example and page 29's CelebA remark, retained as an unmeasured presenter observation.
- Page 35's d = 1000 determinant-complexity illustration and page 41's multiple-trajectory sketch; more explicit Markov, expectation and reverse-factorization steps connect the DDPM derivation.

The tables record the final destinations of the readable technical blocks and sketches. Repeated explanations are consolidated, and printed explanation screenshots are treated as source material. Mathematical corrections and attributed expansions remain distinct from literal source claims.

### Corrections and numerical verification

No source notebook or training code was run. The page 9–10 example was recomputed directly from its probabilities:

- Q(ten heads)=0.5^10=0.0009765625; P(ten heads)=0.99^10≈0.904382.
- Sequence surprisal: −ln Q≈6.931472 nats; −ln P≈0.100503 nats.
- H(P,Q)=−0.99 ln 0.5−0.01 ln 0.5≈0.693147 nats per flip.
- H(Q,P)=−0.5 ln 0.99−0.5 ln 0.01≈2.307610 nats per flip.

These are distinct from the 0.9/0.1 coin's entropy, approximately 0.468996 bits per flip, and H,H,T surprisal, approximately 3.625934 bits. Natural-log and base-2 examples are explicitly separated. EntropyCoin and CrossEntropyBreakdown captions now identify the handwritten notes as their sketch source, preserving all SVG geometry. No new external references were needed for this partition.
