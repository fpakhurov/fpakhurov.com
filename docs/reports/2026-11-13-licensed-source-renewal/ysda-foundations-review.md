## Decisions that affect the renewal

1. `yandexdataschool/nlp_course` branch `2026` is a useful **MIT source candidate at the pinned revision below**, not blanket clearance for every notebook input, figure, PDF, model or linked website. Its checked active files contain substantial third-party material and dependencies.

3. HSE's wiki, the Atakan list and the Kaggle discussion are discovery resources, not reuse grants. Two could not be meaningfully inspected. They cannot supply a rights-cleared replacement merely by being cited.
4. The 80 FP foundations topics (160 EN/RU note files) were screened for source references, explicit adaptation language, executable dataset loaders and imported figures. **They are not all rights-cleared.** Concrete follow-up items include the explicitly adapted VI comparison table, three diabetes examples with unverified dataset permission, and proof/example provenance checks described below.
5. Retain the curriculum structure. Replace or substantiate individual material dependencies; do not delete factual citations to disguise adaptation. The retired xufana material stays withdrawn. New notes must pass the per-file review gate before any BIASED derivation.

### Minimal safe VI replacement proposal

Keep the section and topic, remove its table and its source-following introduction together. Write a new short transition grounded in the course's own previously established equations: (1) an available normalized posterior permits direct posterior summaries; (2) otherwise choose a family `q` and optimize the ELBO; (3) coordinate updates require computing the expected log joint, while stochastic gradients address expectations that cannot be evaluated exactly; (4) EM belongs to the separate choice to optimize point parameters, which can coexist with either exact or variational treatment of latent variables. Link to the existing EM and upcoming BBVI notes.

This was the initial proposal, not a translation or row-by-row rewording of the withdrawn comparison. The implemented alternative below instead uses a newly supplied two-state probability model. The old lecture table was not used to supply its data, wording or explanatory sequence. The implementing reviewer must attach new hashes in a maintenance review record.

The replacement is a newly authored, AI-assisted, two-state example: supplied joint masses 0.12 and 0.08, fixed uniform approximation, then the exact posterior.
