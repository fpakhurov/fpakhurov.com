## Teaching and numerical corrections

The complete bilingual read corrected absolute claims about text spatial control, generator coverage, inversion accuracy, preserved appearance, tracking drift and identity preservation. Mask penalties are soft. The stop-gradient sign argument is local, requires a nonstationary point and small step, and uses a zero subgradient at zero residual. It does not promise monotonic loss or an exact one-pixel movement. The tracked analytic point reaches the **one-pixel threshold**, not the exact target. The schematic's illustrated next point is explicitly not a neural-feature measurement.

DDIM determinism is restricted to eta=0; schedule indices are separated from training indices. The new exact round trip verifies this distinction. Reference-latent attention now uses consistent row-token matrix notation and is a weighted mixture rather than a guaranteed identity copy. The DragBench citation points to Appendix I, and IF is not interpreted as a percentage of preserved pixels. Shared model methods are still explained; no artificial claim of a full trained-model run is made.
