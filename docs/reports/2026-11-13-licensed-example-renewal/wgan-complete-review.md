## Teaching corrections

- The point-mass JS/KL display applies only when `theta != 0`; at zero all discrepancies vanish and W1 is not differentiable. Exact JS behavior is distinguished from finite discriminators and non-saturating generator training.
- Continuity/differentiability and the optimal-critic derivative have explicit regularity/optimality scope. Finite critic training is not claimed to reach the mathematical maximizer.
- Critic-loss/sample-quality correlation is an empirical observation, not a quality guarantee; FID is not an exact transport metric.
- The unit-gradient proposition concerns optimally coupled segments. Random pairing is a heuristic; a finite sampled soft penalty cannot certify a global Lipschitz constraint. Critic updates detach fake samples; generator loss excludes the GP regularizer. Per-image normalization avoids cross-image dependence but does not prove a norm bound.
- The prior approximate-lower-bound claim contradicted the existing synthetic example. Both locales now explicitly show that even the penalized optimum `0.67640625` exceeds the true distance `0.65`.
- Exact nonzero matrix norms, suitable activation slopes and simple-chain composition are distinguished from residual addition, finite power estimates and kernel-reshape proxies. A single power step has no uniform error guarantee. PyTorch behavior is stated in terms of weight access, not assumed once per forward pass.
