## Full-body corrections

- Exact-value TD is a conditional expectation of the return and can reduce variance; arbitrary bootstrap estimates do not inherit that ordering. The wrong-value counterexample makes this limit explicit. Longer horizons/episodes do not universally increase variance or decrease bias.
- MC/TD telescoping is an error identity with a frozen value function, not equality of ordinary online TD(0) and Monte Carlo parameter updates.
- A finite-episode lambda return retains the terminal tail weight. The infinite geometric-series expression only has unit weights for lambda below 1; the endpoint is not obtained by substituting 1 into every coefficient.
- The empirical batch statement concerns a zero of summed residual updates and a discounted, fully specified empirical model. Replaying samples at constant step need not reach that zero, and no unknown-environment dominance is claimed.
- Convergence is scoped to finite stationary tabular discounted MDPs, bounded rewards, coverage and per-entry step-size sums. SARSA additionally needs GLIE for unrestricted optimality; merely epsilon tending to zero is insufficient. Undiscounted absorbing cases require separate hypotheses.
- Constant steps violate the cited diminishing-step conditions but do not rule out convergence in every deterministic special case. The former unqualified exponential/polynomial horizon-rate claim is removed.
- Terminal and time-limit truncation are distinguished. Sample reuse is allowed rather than falsely describing all TD as throwing each transition away.
- The primary Singh publication volume is corrected to 39. Broad lecture/book bibliography entries with no remaining item-specific role were removed; this is not an attempt to retain their examples without attribution.
