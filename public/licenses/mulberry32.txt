Mulberry32 pseudorandom generator

Original algorithm/C implementation: Tommy Ettinger (2017), CC0-1.0.
JavaScript implementation: bryc, public-domain dedication in PRNGs.md.
Local changes: TypeScript seeded closures, variable names and explicit 32-bit
coercions for reproducible teaching illustrations.

Applies to the Mulberry32 implementation in:
  src/components/figures/random.ts
  src/components/figures/geometry.ts
  src/components/figures/em-and-bayesian-pca/bpca.ts
  src/components/figures/variational-inference/ng.ts
It does not license the other algorithms or teaching material in those files.
