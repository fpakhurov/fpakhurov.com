Mulberry32 in the StyleGAN numerical verifier

The uniforms() routine in scripts/notes/check-stylegan-renewal.py adapts the
Mulberry32 JavaScript implementation by bryc. Original algorithm and C
implementation: Tommy Ettinger (2017).
