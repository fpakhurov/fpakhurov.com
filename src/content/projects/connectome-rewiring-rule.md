description: "I wanted to know whether a rule learned from how a nervous system grows could later be useful for learning itself. C. elegans makes the question testable: there are eight complete connectomes of the worm at different developmental stages, from birth to adulthood (Witvliet et al., 2021)."
highlights:
  - "First test: can a rule fitted to developmental synapse formation predict new synapses on neuron pairs it has not seen, and at a later developmental stage?"
  - "Baselines: contact geometry, published models, including a reproduction of the Richter–Schneidman model, and null models."
  - "Second test: the same rule, frozen before any task existed, used as a structural prior in a separate learning experiment."
